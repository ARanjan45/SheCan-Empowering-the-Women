About Us

Global Vision,
Local Action.

We are She Can Foundation — a non-governmental organisation registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860. We empower women and create a more equitable society by providing support, resources, and training — working closely with local organisations, governments, and communities to ensure our programs are effective and sustainable.

Our Story

Founded with Purpose.
Driven by Belief.

"Together, we can break down barriers and empower women. If we all do our part, there is no challenge too great to overcome."

She Can Foundation was born from a simple but powerful belief — that every woman deserves a fair chance. Founded by Reeta Mishra and registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860, the Foundation has grown into a nationwide movement. We believe that by working together, we can revolutionise society and create a better world for all.

The Reality

Did You Know? These are real numbers.

🩸 23M

Girls drop out every year due to menstruation

In India alone, 23 million girls drop out of school annually because of lack of access to sanitary products and menstrual education.

👗 40%

Girls miss school due to lack of proper clothing

Nearly 40% of girls from low-income families report missing school because they don't have adequate or appropriate clothing to wear.

⛓️ 1.5Cr

Girls married before 18 in India every year

India has the highest absolute number of child marriages in the world. Education is the single most powerful tool to end this cycle.

📖 16Cr

Illiterate women in India

160 million women in India cannot read or write. Closing this literacy gap would add $12 trillion to global GDP, per the World Bank.

🛡️ 1 in 3

Women face violence in their lifetime

Globally, 1 in 3 women have experienced physical or sexual violence. Safe spaces, awareness, and support networks save lives.

💻 48%

Women have never used the internet

48% of women in developing countries have never accessed the internet, compared to 35% of men — a digital divide that deepens inequality.

You can change these numbers. Every rupee, every hour, every voice matters.
