Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the internship paid?+
The internship is currently unpaid. However, you receive a verified offer letter, completion certificate, and a performance-based letter of recommendation — which carry real professional value.
Can I volunteer if I'm not a student?+
Absolutely! She Can Foundation welcomes volunteers from all walks of life — students, freshers, working professionals, homemakers. If you have time and passion, there's a place for you.
How do I track where my donation goes?+
All donors receive an impact update within 30 days of their donation, sharing how the funds were used. We are fully transparent and committed to accountability.
Is there a minimum donation amount?+
There is no minimum. Even ₹10 is welcome. Every rupee goes directly to on-ground programs supporting women and girls across India.
Are the distress messages really kept confidential?+
Yes, absolutely. Messages sent via our distress form are read only by our trained core team. We never share any personal information without explicit consent. If you choose to remain anonymous, we fully respect that.
How do I become a campus ambassador?+
Apply via the Volunteer page and select "Campus Ambassador" as your role. We'll reach out within 3 days with onboarding details. Ambassadors receive official certificates, LOR opportunities, and special recognition.
